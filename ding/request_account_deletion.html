Account management

Delete your account

We're sorry to see you go. If you no longer want to use Ding Messenger, you can permanently delete your account using either of the methods below.

01

Delete from the Ding app

Open Ding Messenger on your device, navigate to Settings, and tap Delete account. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the deletion process.

02

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, send an account deletion request from your registered email address. Include your registered phone number with the country code so we can identify your account.

Request account deletion →

What happens when you delete your account?

Your account will be scheduled for deletion after your request is verified. Account-associated data will be deleted in accordance with our data retention practices and applicable legal requirements. Some information may be retained where legally required.
